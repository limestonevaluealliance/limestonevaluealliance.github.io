Who We Are

Limestone Value Alliance Plc is a proudly Nigerian industrial company delivering complete limestone value-chain solutions — from quarry to finished products. We mine, process, refine, manufacture, package, and distribute limestone and limestone-derived products for industrial, agricultural, construction, and environmental markets. Our strength lies in vertical integration, enabling us to control every stage of production to ensure quality, efficiency, reliability, and competitive pricing.

Our Mission

To become a leading African building materials and industrial minerals brand serving global markets.

Our Vision

To deliver consistent, high-quality limestone solutions while creating sustainable value for customers, partners, communities, and shareholders.
